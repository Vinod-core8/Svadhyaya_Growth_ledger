Executive Summary

Vriddhi Games sells a single flagship product — "Guess Who Am I," a 54-card heritage game built around the heroes and sages of the Itihasas and Puranas. It's a low price-point, high-margin, gifting- and education-led product, well suited to paid social discovery and targeted search intent.

This document outlines a lean, trackable paid media setup across Meta and Google for a monthly budget of ₹8,000–₹10,000, split 70% Meta / 30% Google. At this budget, the objective is not aggressive scale — it is to identify the winning audience and creative angle on each platform, establish reliable performance data, and build a foundation to scale confidently in subsequent months.

06

Conclusion & Key Recommendations

1

The 70/30 Meta–Google split fits this product well: Meta captures impulse and emotion-driven demand, while Google captures the smaller pool of people already searching for it.

2

Keep both channels lean, not fragmented — 2–3 Meta ad sets and one focused Google Search campaign will consistently outperform spreading the budget across five or more campaigns.

3

Retargeting delivers the most cost-efficient conversions on the account and should be protected even if other budgets are trimmed.

4

Creative is the primary lever at this spend, more so than audience targeting — a strong UGC, testimonial, or festival-themed ad will outperform incremental targeting adjustments.

5

Align creative refreshes with the Hindu festival calendar — Diwali, Janmashtami, Dussehra, and Ram Navami represent natural gifting spikes for this product.

6

Hold off on Performance Max until the account has logged at least 10 conversions — Search alone is the better use of ₹3,000/month for now.

7

Review performance weekly rather than daily — small budgets need a few days per ad set to generate enough signal for a fair read.
