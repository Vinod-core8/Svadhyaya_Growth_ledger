Ledger · 02

Content Workflow — Step by Step

The same loop, every week, right up to the 21st. Nothing gets posted without moving through all eight steps.

1
Ideate against the pillar planSunday planning
Pick the week's topics from the pillar mix above — never freestyle a topic that doesn't map to a pillar.
2
Script the hook and the payoffWriter
First line written before anything else — if the hook doesn't earn the scroll-stop, the topic gets reworked.
3
Shoot / design in one batchProduction
All of the week's Reels recorded with Shweta in a single sitting; posters designed against the pillar brief.
4
Edit & captionEditor
Subtitles on by default, CTA written into the caption, not left to the comments.
5
Shweta approvesClient sign-off
One approval pass per batch — accuracy check on the accountancy content, tone check on the hook.
6
Schedule & publishCross-platform
Reel → Instagram, repurposed same day to YouTube Shorts and Facebook.
7
Engage for the first 2 hoursCommunity
Reply to every comment and DM fast — this window decides how far the algorithm pushes it, and every course question becomes a direct enquiry.
8
Report & feed back into next weekWeekly review
Best performer gets a boost or a sequel; weak performer's format gets dropped, not repeated blindly.
